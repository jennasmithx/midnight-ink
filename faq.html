Before You Book

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything people usually ask before their first session. Can't find your answer? WhatsApp us — we're happy to help.

How old do I need to be?

You must be 18 or older, with no exceptions. Please bring a valid ID (ID book or card, driver's licence or passport) to your consultation and to your session.

Do you take walk‑ins?

No — Midnight Ink is by appointment only. Every tattoo starts with a consultation so we can design something custom for you. Send an enquiry or WhatsApp us to get started.

How does booking and the deposit work?

After your consultation we confirm a date and a quote. A non‑refundable deposit of 30% (minimum R500) secures the appointment and comes off the final price. It covers design time. Full details are on the Pricing page.

What if I need to reschedule?

Let us know as early as you can and we'll move your booking. Late cancellations and no‑shows forfeit the deposit, because that time and design work can't be given to someone else.

How much will my tattoo cost?

It depends on size, placement and detail. Our Pricing page shows starting prices, and you'll get a firm quote at your consultation — before the needle touches skin. The studio minimum is R650 per session.

Can I bring my own design or reference pictures?

Yes, please do. Reference images help us understand the style you like. We don't copy another artist's tattoo, but we'll use your references to design something original for you.

Do you do cover‑ups or rework old tattoos?

Often, yes — but it depends on how dark and large the old tattoo is. Bring it to a consultation and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

Does it hurt?

A bit — it varies by person and placement. Fleshier areas like the outer arm and thigh tend to be easier; ribs, feet, hands and spine are more sensitive. We take breaks whenever you need them.

How should I prepare for my session?

Get a good night's sleep, eat a proper meal beforehand, and drink plenty of water. Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before. Wear comfortable clothing that gives easy access to the placement. Bring snacks and something to keep you busy for longer sessions.

Can I get tattooed if I'm pregnant, breastfeeding or on medication?

We don't tattoo during pregnancy or while breastfeeding. If you have a medical condition, take blood thinners, or have a skin condition in the area, mention it when you enquire — we may ask you to check with your doctor first.

How long does healing take?

The surface usually heals in 2–4 weeks, with the deeper skin settling over a couple of months. Our Aftercare guide walks you through it step by step.

Can I bring a friend?

One support person is welcome. Our studio is small and private, so please ask before bringing anyone else — and no children in the studio.

When is the mobile studio coming?

From 2027 Midnight Ink will tour festivals around South Africa. Tour dates will be announced once confirmed — use the Get Notified button on the home page to hear first.
