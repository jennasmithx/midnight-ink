Deposits

A non‑refundable deposit of 30% (minimum R500) secures your appointment and is deducted from the final price of your piece. Deposits cover design time and are forfeited on late cancellations or no‑shows.

Minimum Charge

Our studio minimum is R650 per session, regardless of size — this covers setup, sterilisation and artist time for even the smallest custom piece.

Touch‑Ups

One complimentary touch‑up is included within 6 weeks of your original session for pieces R1,800 and up. Touch‑ups outside this window are charged at a reduced flat rate. More questions? See the FAQ.
